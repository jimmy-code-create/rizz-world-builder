---
name: RedNote media embeds
description: The current support boundary for displaying RedNote videos inside RIZZ.
---

As of 2026-10-03, the public RedNote developer documentation located for this work describes seller and product integrations, not a public third-party video embed player. Unofficial downloader APIs exist but are not an approved substitute.

**Why:** RIZZ's external media flow must use officially permitted player/embed mechanisms and must not download or redistribute third-party videos.

**How to apply:** Before enabling RedNote in-app playback, verify current official documentation or approved partner access. Preserve the official player and attribution; keep link publishing blocked when no approved embed mechanism is available.
